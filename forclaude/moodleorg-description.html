How well are your students reading?

Poodll ReadAloud is a guided reading-fluency and reading-comprehension activity for Moodle. The teacher sets a passage; the student works through a structured journey to read it, and ReadAloud grades the attempt automatically — words per minute, accuracy, and (optionally) a full follow-up quiz about the passage.

The student journey

A ReadAloud activity is built from a reading passage plus a set of steps, which the teacher turns on or off:

  • Listen — the student listens to the passage before attempting it, using audio you supply or automatically generated text-to-speech.
  • Practice (Listen and Repeat) — the passage is broken into lines with model audio for each, and the student practices reading along line by line.
  • Read — the student reads the whole passage aloud while recording. This is the graded attempt.
  • Quiz — an optional set of follow-up questions about the passage.
  • Report — students and teachers see the results: WPM, accuracy, the marked-up transcript, and AI feedback if enabled.
Automatic, tunable grading

Reading attempts are scored automatically using speech recognition: words per minute against a target you set, and accuracy against the passage text. Mismatches between the transcription and the original passage are shown to the student — valuable feedback for pronunciation practice, and students can reattempt to improve. Grading can be tuned per activity:

  • Alternatives — specify acceptable mistranscriptions for tricky words (e.g. their / there / they're) so near-homophones aren't marked wrong.
  • WPM adjust (AI) — choose whether reading errors are fully subtracted from the score, discounted by a fixed amount, or ignored.
  • Guided vs. open speech-to-text — match transcription against the expected passage, or transcribe openly.
  • Human or machine grading — let AI grade automatically, or have a teacher review and adjust.
An optional quiz, right after the passage

Turn on the Quiz step and ReadAloud adds a set of follow-up questions about the passage — the same kind of item-based quiz mechanism used in Poodll MiniLesson, built into ReadAloud directly. It turns a fluency check into a complete comprehension activity, without leaving the page.

Built for a real classroom
  • Model Audio tab — record or generate the line-by-line audio used in Practice mode; it's split into sentence clips automatically.
  • Master instance / Push — designate one ReadAloud as a master and push its settings to other ReadAloud activities of the same name across your site.
  • Native language feedback — students can choose their own language for AI feedback on the quiz's writing and speaking items.
  • Over 60 speech recognition / text-to-speech languages and accents, including English, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese, Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Arabic, Hindi and many more.
  • Runs in the Moodle mobile app.
Use Cases

Native Speakers
In first-language contexts, ReadAloud is most often used for reading practice, assessment, and progress monitoring. Many educational authorities require students to reach a certain level of reading proficiency before advancing to the next grade. ReadAloud makes it easy for teachers to collect measures of oral reading ability that can be shared with parents, administrators, and other stakeholders.

Language Learners
ReadAloud is also used extensively in language learning. Curricula that include extensive reading or speed reading programs use it to measure changes in reading speed and accuracy via pre- and post-testing, and the follow-up quiz turns a reading check into a full comprehension and vocabulary activity.

Getting Started

ReadAloud requires a Poodll API username and secret. These are available with a free trial or a paid subscription. To get yours:

  1. Visit: https://poodll.com/try-poodll
  2. Follow the instructions to make your Poodll account and take a free trial
  3. Register your Moodle site URL and pick up your API user and secret.

You can install Poodll ReadAloud as usual from the Moodle plugins directory. After installation you will be asked for your API user and secret. Please see the Poodll ReadAloud support documentation for more information on installing and using Read Aloud.
